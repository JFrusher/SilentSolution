# Control-room models

Every model here is generated by `src/graphics/models.py` and kept out of git. The game builds any missing ones when
the room first opens, and `build.py` builds them before packaging. Rebuild them all (overwriting your own) with
`PYTHONPATH=src uv run src/graphics/models.py`. They are binary glTF (`.glb`) with their textures embedded, and
`src/graphics/gltf.py` reads them.

## Dropping in a better model

You can replace any file with your own `.glb` under the same name: a CC0 crewman, for example. The reader takes:

- triangles, with **normals**; uv, `COLOR_0` and one base-colour texture are optional
- metallic/roughness and emissive factors
- node transforms (TRS or matrix) and node hierarchies

It refuses with a clear error: other primitive modes, sparse accessors, and external `.bin` buffers.

**Units and axes:** metres, +Y up.

- **A station** (`stations/*.glb`):
  - Its origin is the floor under the centre of its panel. +Z points out of the panel into the room, and +X is to the right as you face it.
  - The live screen is drawn by the game at the panel's position, so keep the panel's rectangle clear: 18° back from vertical, centred at the height in `src/control_room.py`.
- **A crewman** (`crew/*.glb`):
  - His origin is the floor under his seat, and he faces +Z.
  - Name the node that carries his head `head`, with its origin at the neck. The game turns that node to make him look about and face you when he speaks.

If you add a third-party model, credit it in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.
